Taskado Privacy Policy

Taskado (developed by Team 45) is a student-led university project designed to help users manage tasks, events, habits, notes, and productivity data. We are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines what data we collect, how and why we collect it, how it is used and stored, and your rights under applicable data protection laws.

Topics:

  • Who we are
  • What data we collect
  • How we collect your data
  • How we use your data
  • How we store and protect your data
  • Your data protection rights
  • Changes to this policy
  • How to contact us
  • Supervisory authority contact

Who we are

Taskado is operated by Team 45, a student team at the University of Birmingham, for academic purposes as part of the 2024–2025 team project module. We act as the data controllers for information collected via our application.

What data we collect

  • Account information (e.g., username, email address)
  • Usage data (e.g., task completions, habits tracked, reminder settings)
  • Technical data (e.g., device information, IP address, browser type)
  • Accessibility and appearance preferences

How we collect your data

We collect data when you:

  • Register or log into your account
  • Interact with core app features (tasks, events, habits, notes, reminders)
  • Adjust your accessibility or appearance settings
  • Submit feedback via forms or email

How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and personalise your dashboard experience
  • Deliver system notifications and reminders
  • Improve app usability and functionality
  • Respond to technical support requests or feedback

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties outside of the University of Birmingham project scope.

How we store and protect your data

  • Data is stored in secure PostgreSQL databases hosted via Render.com.
  • Version control and project code are secured via GitHub private repositories.
  • Only authorized Taskado developers (Team 45) have access to your data.
  • Data will be retained until the project ends in Summer 2025, after which it will be securely deleted.

Your data protection rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you have rights including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to rectify incorrect or incomplete data
  • The right to request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability (transfer your data)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email addresses provided below.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in project scope or legal requirements. All changes will be communicated through the application. Last updated: 28 April 2025.
